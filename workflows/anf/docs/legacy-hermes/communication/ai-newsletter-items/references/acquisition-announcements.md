# Acquisition-announcement verification

Use this for same-day AI-company acquisition ANFs, especially when rumors or “near deal” coverage preceded a confirmed announcement.

## Workflow

1. **Separate rumor from signed transaction.** Search for same-day primary announcements from both companies and confirmed coverage. Do not use an earlier “reportedly,” “nears,” or “in talks” article as the final URL after the parties confirm the deal.
2. **Read the transaction language.** Distinguish “agreed to acquire” or “joining” from a completed acquisition. Record closing conditions, expected timing, disclosed or undisclosed financial terms, and any regulatory caveats.
3. **Verify continuity claims.** Report commitments about product name, integrations, roadmap, routing neutrality, staff, or independence only when the announcement states them. Frame these as company commitments, not guaranteed outcomes.
4. **Handle blocked official domains.** If the primary announcement’s hostname is forbidden for the final URL (for example, a `.ai` domain), use exact same-event confirmed coverage as `URL:`. A story-specific image from the official announcement may still be used when it passes the separate image policy.
5. **Prefer current imagery.** Use the acquisition announcement’s story-specific hero image. Reject old generic publisher OpenGraph images even when the article itself is the strongest compliant final URL.

## Example source alignment

For a confirmed OpenRouter–Stripe agreement, an earlier article saying Stripe would “reportedly acquire” OpenRouter establishes historical context but is not the final confirmation source. The final item should cite same-day confirmed coverage, describe the transaction as pending customary closing conditions, preserve the expected closing window, and note that financial terms were not disclosed if confirmed by the selected source.
