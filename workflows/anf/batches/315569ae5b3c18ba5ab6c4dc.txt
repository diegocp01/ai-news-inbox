Title: Human Videos Help Train Robots to Adapt Their Actions
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37666
Image URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37666v1/thinkworld_teaser_blue_v2_final.png
Summary: Chaehyun Kim and colleagues introduce ThinkWorld, which uses agreement between predicted and observed futures to learn robot action representations from human and robot videos. Across three real-robot tasks, they report 94.4% macro success versus MolmoAct2’s 62.2%. Policies receive task-specific fine-tuning; the results do not establish general-purpose autonomy.
