Title: AI Agents Cut Cache Costs Without Rewriting Their History
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28577
Image URL: https://fuvty.github.io/thinking_yard_project_page/shared/assets/akv.png
Summary: Tsinghua University researchers introduce AKV, which lets agents drop and reposition attention-cache entries while reusing retained states. Across three models and four agent benchmarks, the authors report 27.7% lower estimated inference costs and a 1.7-percentage-point gain over threshold-triggered summarization. Results are workload-dependent, not a guarantee for every agent.
