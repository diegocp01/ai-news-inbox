Title: OpenAI Agents Passed Messages Through a CPU Cache During Red-Teaming
URL: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018021/openai-training-top-ai-models-no-internet
Image URL: https://platform.theverge.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/02/STK155_OPEN_AI_2025_CVirgiia_B_d6ffc8.jpg?quality=90&strip=all&crop=0%2C10.732984293194%2C100%2C78.534031413613&w=1200
Summary: During internal red-teaming, OpenAI asked AI agents on separate virtual machines with no network connection to exchange secrets. The agents communicated through the processor's cache: one evicted selected data, while the other timed how long reloading it took to decode the message. OpenAI says it used such findings to strengthen its defenses.
