Title: Google Research Proposes a "Sleep" Phase to Help LLMs Keep Learning
URL: https://research.google/blog/the-sleep-framework-a-new-way-for-llms-to-retain-knowledge-in-continual-learning/
Image URL: https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-research2023-media/images/HO_previewImage1.width-800.format-jpeg.jpg
Summary: Google Research introduced a "sleep" framework for continual learning, inspired by how the brain consolidates memory during rest. LLMs alternate active interaction with offline phases that move short-term context into long-term parameters. Google says this lets models keep acquiring new facts, retain long-context knowledge up to 10M tokens and improve reasoning while mitigating catastrophic forgetting.
