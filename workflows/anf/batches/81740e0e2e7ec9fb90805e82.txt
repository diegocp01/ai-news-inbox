Title: World Models Estimate Alternative Outcomes Without Retraining
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24778
Image URL: https://api.alphaxiv.org/open-graph/v1/paper/2611.24778.png?v=2
Summary: Randall Balestriero and colleagues reuse an observed outcome’s prediction residual to estimate what a different action would have produced under the same disturbance. Across four rendered simulators, they report counterfactual R² increasing from 38–46% to 86–96%, without retraining the predictor. Results are simulation-only; contacts and action-dependent disturbances can invalidate the approximation.
